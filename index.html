Welcome Home

You Belong
Here With Us

At Living Word Church, we believe the heart of everything is family. Whether you're visiting for the first time or have been with us for years, you'll find a warm place where God's love is present in every handshake, every song, and every message.

Rooted in Adairville, Kentucky, we are a church that brings generations together — united by a shared passion for authentic worship and God's living Word.

Praise & Worship

Worship Is
Who We Are

At Living Word, worship isn't just a song — it's a lifestyle. Every service we come together as family to encounter God's presence through dynamic praise, powerful teaching, and Spirit-led moments that change lives.

We believe the church is not a building — it's a people. A people gathered around the Word, loving each other fiercely, and reaching Adairville and beyond.
