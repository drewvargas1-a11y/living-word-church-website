Jail Ministry

Meet the Leader

Driven by a calling to bring hope and the gospel to those behind the walls.

Jail Ministry Leader

John Paul Boisseau

Passionate about carrying the gospel to the places most people overlook — including those behind bars.

John Paul leads our Jail Ministry with the conviction that no one is too far gone for God's grace. He goes into local correctional facilities to share the Word, pray with inmates, and remind them that they are not forgotten — by God or by this community.
