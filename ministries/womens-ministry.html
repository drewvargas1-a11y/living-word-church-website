Who We Are

Women Rooted in Faith,
Walking Together

Our Women's Ministry is open to every woman at Living Word — no matter where you are in your walk with God, what season of life you're in, or how long you've been coming to church.

We gather throughout the year for events designed to encourage, equip, and connect. From intimate dinners to larger celebrations, each gathering is a chance to deepen friendships, grow in the Word, and be reminded that you are not walking this road alone.
