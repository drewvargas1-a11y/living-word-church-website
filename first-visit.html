What We're About

Five Pillars of Our Community

Everything we do at Living Word Church flows from these five commitments. They shape how we gather, how we grow, and how we serve.

🙌

Worship

We believe worship is more than music — it's a lifestyle. Our Sunday gatherings are filled with Spirit-led praise, heartfelt prayer, and an atmosphere where people encounter the presence of God.

📖

Learning

We're committed to Bible-grounded teaching that is practical, relevant, and life-changing. From Sunday sermons to mid-week studies, we dig into the Word together so everyone can grow in faith.

🤝

Enrichment

Growth happens in community. Through discipleship groups, ministry programs, and special events, we help every person move forward in their walk with God — wherever they're starting from.

❤️

Friendship

We are a family. You won't be a stranger here for long. Our church is a place where genuine friendships are built, where people show up for each other, and where nobody walks through life alone.

🌍

Service

We believe the Church exists not for itself, but for the world. We actively serve our local community in Adairville and support global missions to bring hope and the Gospel to those who need it most.

What We Believe

Our Statement of Faith

As a nondenominational, Spirit-filled Christian church, these are the core beliefs that define who we are and how we live.

01

The Trinity

We believe in one God, eternally existing in three persons: Father, Son, and Holy Spirit — co-equal and co-eternal.

02

Salvation

We believe that all people are sinners and that salvation comes by grace alone, through faith alone, in Jesus Christ alone. It is a free gift — not earned by works.

03

The Bible

We believe the Bible is the inspired, infallible Word of God and the final authority in all matters of faith, life, and practice.

04

The Resurrection

We believe in the bodily resurrection of Jesus Christ from the dead, and in the resurrection of all the dead — believers to eternal life, unbelievers to judgment.

05

Divine Healing

We believe that divine healing is provided in the atonement of Christ and is available to all who believe through prayer and faith in Jesus' name.

06

Baptism of the Holy Spirit

We believe in the baptism of the Holy Spirit, with the initial evidence of speaking in other tongues, as described in Acts 2 and available to all believers today.

07

The Second Coming

We believe in the literal, physical, and imminent return of Jesus Christ to establish His eternal kingdom.

08

Evangelism

We believe in the Great Commission — the call to go into all the world and preach the Gospel to every person, making disciples of all nations.

09

Sanctification

We believe in the sanctifying power of the Holy Spirit — the ongoing work of being set apart and transformed into Christlikeness in thought, word, and deed.

10

The Church

We believe in the local church as the Body of Christ — a community of believers called together for worship, fellowship, discipleship, and service to the world.

Transparency & Trust

Accountability at Living Word

We believe healthy leadership is shared leadership. At Living Word Church, a board of advisors works alongside the pastor to ensure that no single person holds unchecked authority over the church — spiritually or financially.

🏛️

Board of Advisors

Our board of advisors is made up of trusted men of faith who bring wisdom, experience, and a heart for God's people. They serve as a checks-and-balances structure for the church — reviewing major decisions, guiding direction, and ensuring everything is handled with integrity.

💰

Financial Accountability

Every dollar given to Living Word Church is handled with full transparency and stewardship. Financial decisions — including budgets, expenditures, and major purchases — require oversight and approval from the board of advisors, not a single individual.

  • ✓ Board review of all major financial decisions
  • ✓ No single person controls the church's finances
  • ✓ Giving is stewarded for ministry, community, and missions
🙏

Spiritual Accountability

We believe the Bible calls leaders to be accountable to one another. The pastor and church leadership submit to the guidance and wisdom of the board of advisors, ensuring that our spiritual direction stays rooted in Scripture and sound doctrine.

  • ✓ Shared decision-making across leadership
  • ✓ Counsel sought before major directional changes
  • ✓ Leadership held to the standard of God's Word

"Plans fail for lack of counsel, but with many advisers they succeed."

Proverbs 15:22
